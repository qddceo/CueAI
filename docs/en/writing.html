✍️ Writing

Good for weekly reports, marketing copy, long-form drafts, translation, and polish. Compared below by positioning / fit / not a fit / starting price.

ChatGPT

From: free tier · Plus ~$20/mo

In one line: general writing assistant for drafting, rewriting, brainstorming, and multilingual work.

Best for

Everyday copy, email, outlines, iterative edits; plugins / browsing or custom GPTs.

Not ideal for

High-stakes factual writing that needs auditable citations (always human-verify).

Website

Claude

From: free tier · Pro ~$20/mo

In one line: strong at long-form, structured writing, and careful revision; large context window.

Best for

Long reports, proposals, contract draft polish, tone consistency from style guides.

Not ideal for

Workflows that require heavy live web search or a locked plugin marketplace.

Website

Notion AI

From: add-on to Notion workspace (per seat)

In one line: write, summarize, and translate inside Notion pages—great if your wiki already lives there.

Best for

Team wikis, meeting notes, summaries and action items from existing pages.

Not ideal for

You don’t use Notion, or you need a standalone multimodal / coding powerhouse.

Website

Grammarly

From: free tier · Premium yearly

In one line: English spelling, grammar, and tone checks as you write in the browser or docs.

Best for

English email, resumes, blogs—clarity and correctness.

Not ideal for

Chinese-first creation, or generating long content from scratch (it’s editing, not authorship).

Website
