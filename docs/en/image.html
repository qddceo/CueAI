Text-to-image, concept design, illustration, and marketing assets. Check each platform’s copyright and commercial terms.

Midjourney

From: subscription (no long-term free tier)

In one line: text-to-image known for artistic feel and style consistency (Discord / Web).

Best for

Concept art, brand mood boards, illustration exploration; iterating prompts in Discord or Web.

Not ideal for

Pixel-perfect layout, strict brand fonts embedded, or fully offline local deploy.

Website

ChatGPT (Images)

From: ChatGPT plans (limited on free)

In one line: conversational image create/edit—say what you need and iterate lightly.

Best for

Quick drafts, icon/illustration trials, combined with writing tasks.

Not ideal for

Industrial batch pipelines, or teams that need local open-model fine-tuning.

Website

Leonardo.Ai

From: free credits · paid monthly

In one line: game/asset-leaning text-to-image with many models and workflows.

Best for

Characters, props, textures; trying many models without self-hosting.

Not ideal for

Users who only want “one prompt → one poster” and dislike parameter-heavy UIs.

Website

Adobe Firefly

From: free credits · Creative Cloud plans

In one line: generative images inside Adobe—aimed at design work and commercial compliance.

Best for

Designers already on Photoshop / Express; teams that care about training data & licensing.

Not ideal for

Anyone avoiding Adobe accounts, or chasing the most “internet anime” look.
