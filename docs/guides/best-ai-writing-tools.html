Best AI writing tools: how to choose

There is no single best writing AI. Pick by the job: a first draft in chat, a long structured revision, writing inside a team wiki, or English proofreading on top of text you already have.

What to decide first

  • Draft or edit. Chat tools are for starting and rewriting. Grammarly is for correcting English you already wrote.
  • Length and structure. Short email and marketing copy are a general chat job. Long reports, proposals, and tone that has to stay consistent favor a model that handles long context well.
  • Where the words live. If the source of truth is a Notion workspace, writing inside those pages beats copying text in and out of a separate chat.
  • Language. A Chinese-first draft and an English grammar pass are different products. Do not expect a proofreader to author a long piece from a blank page.
  • Facts. High-stakes claims still need a human check. None of these tools replace a cited source.

Starting prices below are the same ranges already listed on the writing directory. They change; confirm them on each official site before you pay.

ChatGPT

From: free tier · Plus ~$20/mo

In one line: general writing assistant for drafting, rewriting, brainstorming, and multilingual work.

Best for

Everyday copy, email, outlines, iterative edits; plugins / browsing or custom GPTs.

Not ideal for

High-stakes factual writing that needs auditable citations (always human-verify).

Website

Claude

From: free tier · Pro ~$20/mo

In one line: strong at long-form, structured writing, and careful revision; large context window.

Best for

Long reports, proposals, contract draft polish, tone consistency from style guides.

Not ideal for

Workflows that require heavy live web search or a locked plugin marketplace.

Website

Notion AI

From: add-on to Notion workspace (per seat)

In one line: write, summarize, and translate inside Notion pages—great if your wiki already lives there.

Best for

Team wikis, meeting notes, summaries and action items from existing pages.

Not ideal for

You don’t use Notion, or you need a standalone multimodal / coding powerhouse.

Website

Grammarly

From: free tier · Premium yearly

In one line: English spelling, grammar, and tone checks as you write in the browser or docs.

Best for

English email, resumes, blogs—clarity and correctness.

Not ideal for

Chinese-first creation, or generating long content from scratch (it’s editing, not authorship).

Website

A practical shortlist

  • Weekly copy, email, and outlines: start with ChatGPT, then switch if the draft is long and you care more about structure than plugins.
  • Reports, proposals, and style-guide rewrites: try Claude on the same brief before you pay for a second subscription.
  • Notes that already live in Notion: use Notion AI so summaries stay next to the source pages.
  • English that only needs to be cleaner: Grammarly, and keep a chat tool for the blank-page work.
