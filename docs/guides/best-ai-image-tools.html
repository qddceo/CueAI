Best AI image tools: how to choose

“Best AI for images” depends on the deliverable: a mood board, a quick illustration next to a paragraph, a game asset, or a file that has to live inside an existing design suite. Copyright and commercial terms are part of the choice, not a footnote.

What to decide first

  • Look versus control. Artistic exploration and style consistency are a different job from pixel-perfect layout, embedded brand fonts, or a poster that must match a grid.
  • How you want to iterate. Some tools expect prompt rounds in Discord or on the web. Others sit in a chat you already use for writing. Others expose many models and parameters.
  • Where the file goes next. If the team already designs in Photoshop or Express, generating inside that suite avoids a second export workflow.
  • Licensing. Read the current commercial terms on the vendor’s site before you ship client work. This guide does not summarize a license you have not read.
  • Volume. A handful of drafts is not an industrial batch pipeline, and a hosted product is not a locally fine-tuned open model.

Starting prices below match the image directory. Several vendors publish credits or plan names rather than one public monthly number, so those cards stay qualitative.

In one line: text-to-image known for artistic feel and style consistency (Discord / Web).

Best for

Concept art, brand mood boards, illustration exploration; iterating prompts in Discord or Web.

Not ideal for

Pixel-perfect layout, strict brand fonts embedded, or fully offline local deploy.

In one line: game/asset-leaning text-to-image with many models and workflows.

Best for

Characters, props, textures; trying many models without self-hosting.

Not ideal for

Users who only want “one prompt → one poster” and dislike parameter-heavy UIs.

Adobe Firefly

From: free credits · Creative Cloud plans

In one line: generative images inside Adobe—aimed at design work and commercial compliance.

Best for

Designers already on Photoshop / Express; teams that care about training data & licensing.

Not ideal for

Anyone avoiding Adobe accounts, or chasing the most “internet anime” look.

Website

A practical shortlist

  • Concept art and mood boards, and you will iterate prompts: Midjourney. Budget for a subscription; there is no long-term free tier on the directory card.
  • A draft image while you are already writing: ChatGPT images, then move the keeper into a design tool.
  • Characters, props, and textures, and you want several models without hosting them: Leonardo.Ai.
  • The file has to land in Photoshop or Express, and licensing is part of the brief: Adobe Firefly, then confirm the current commercial terms yourself.
