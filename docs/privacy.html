Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-29 · Operator: Duo Qian / CueAI (https://www.ztlab.art)

CueAI is a static directory of AI tools by use case. We collect as little personal information as practical. This policy explains how we handle information related to the site.

What we collect

  • Browsing: Pages are mostly static. Unless we later enable analytics, we do not actively collect your name, email, or account data on our servers.
  • Contacting us: If you email qddceo@mail.grokbot.com, we process what you send in order to reply.
  • Payments & sponsorship: If you pay via Creem for digital goods or sponsorship, payment and billing data are handled by Creem (Merchant of Record) under its privacy policy. We may receive non-sensitive order details (e.g. email, product, amount) for fulfillment and support.
  • Affiliate links: When you follow outbound links with affiliate parameters, vendors or their networks may set cookies or attribution data under their policies. We do not control those third parties.
  • Local preferences: Theme (light/dark) is stored in your browser’s localStorage for display only and is not uploaded to our servers.

How we use information

Only to operate the site, answer inquiries, deliver purchased digital content, and improve the directory. We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

We may link to third-party sites and payment services (including Creem). Review their privacy policies. Hosting/CDN providers such as GitHub Pages / Cloudflare may process technical access logs (e.g. IP, User-Agent) under their terms.

Retention & your rights

Email and order-related records are kept as long as needed for the business. You may email to request access, correction, or deletion of information we hold about you (except where law requires retention).
