About CueAI

Cite-able facts · Updated: 2026-10-29

What is CueAI?

CueAI is a free directory of AI tools organized by use case. It groups publicly available AI tools into scenarios—writing, image, video, coding, office, and customer support—so visitors can choose by task rather than by brand leaderboards. The site URL is https://www.ztlab.art.

Who runs it?

CueAI is operated by Duo Qian, under the ztlab brand/site. It is an independent directory and is not owned by any single AI tool vendor unless a page states otherwise.

What it offers

  • Scenario pages for writing, image, video, coding, office, and support
  • Chinese and English pages, plus light/dark theme toggle
  • Primarily static pages; browsing the directory is free

Monetization (optional)

Using the directory is free. Optional support includes:

  • Creem sponsorships / digital checkout: Visitors may pay on the sponsor page via Creem (Merchant of Record); Creem handles payments and tax.
  • Affiliate links: Some outbound links may include affiliate parameters. If you buy through them, CueAI may earn a commission, typically without changing your price. The site discloses affiliate links.

Contact

Languages and affiliation

The site is available in Chinese (zh-CN) and English (en). CueAI / ztlab.art is not affiliated with or endorsed by listed tool vendors unless a page says so. Features, pricing, and availability follow each product’s official documentation.
